Flexi Hybrid Loan Guide

What is a Flexi Hybrid Loan?

A Flexi Hybrid Loan is a flexible loan facility that combines lower initial EMI with the convenience of a revolving credit limit. During the initial tenure, borrowers may pay a lower EMI compared to a traditional personal loan, helping improve cash flow management.

Unlike a standard personal loan, a Flexi Hybrid Loan allows borrowers to withdraw funds from the available limit whenever required and make part-prepayments without permanently reducing access to funds. Interest is generally charged only on the utilized amount, which can help reduce overall interest costs when managed effectively.

This loan structure is often preferred by salaried employees, self-employed professionals and business owners who require funding flexibility while maintaining better control over monthly repayments.

Interest Calculation

How Interest is Calculated in a Flexi Hybrid Loan

One of the biggest advantages of a Flexi Hybrid Loan is that interest is generally charged only on the amount you have utilized, rather than the entire approved loan limit.

For example, if a lender approves a Flexi Hybrid Loan limit of ₹10,00,000 and you withdraw only ₹4,00,000, interest is typically calculated on the utilized amount of ₹4,00,000 instead of the full approved limit.

If you make part-prepayments, your utilized amount reduces, which may help reduce future interest costs. Depending on lender policies, you may also be able to withdraw funds again from the available limit whenever required.

This flexible structure can help borrowers manage cash flow more efficiently compared to traditional personal loans where interest is generally charged on the entire disbursed amount.
