7 Warning Signs Your Debt May Be Becoming Difficult To Manage

If you identify with several of these situations, it may be worth reviewing your current repayment strategy and exploring options that can help improve cash flow.

1. Paying Only Minimum Due on Credit Cards

Your outstanding balance continues growing even though you make payments every month.

2. Using New Loans to Repay Existing EMIs

Borrowing repeatedly to manage current repayments can create a debt cycle.

3. More Than 40% Of Salary Goes Towards EMIs

A high EMI-to-income ratio can make monthly budgeting difficult.

4. Missing Due Dates Frequently

Multiple payment dates increase the chances of accidental delays.

5. No Emergency Savings Left

Most of your income is being used to service debt obligations.

6. Constant Stress About EMI Payments

Financial pressure starts affecting personal and professional life.

7. Multiple Credit Cards Are Near Their Limits

High utilization on credit cards may affect financial flexibility and future borrowing options.

If 3 or more signs apply to you, it may be worth exploring debt consolidation or EMI reduction options.
