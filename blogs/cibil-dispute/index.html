How to Raise a CIBIL Dispute Online in 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

Updated on Aug 16, 2026 · By TechFinserv Team

Introduction

Your CIBIL report plays an important role when you apply for a loan or credit card. But sometimes, it may contain incorrect information such as a wrong loan account, payment status, outstanding balance, personal details, or duplicate account.

If you find an error in your CIBIL report, you can raise a CIBIL dispute online and request the information to be reviewed and corrected. In this guide, we’ll show you the complete CIBIL dispute process step by step, with screenshots of each stage to make it easy to follow.

How to Raise a Dispute on CIBIL: Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Log in to Your CIBIL Account

Login to Your CIBIL Account ↗

First, visit the official CIBIL website and log in using your registered username and password.

Enter your registered username and password, then click on the Login button.

After logging in, you may be asked to verify your identity using an OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

💡
Tip:

Use the same registered email ID or mobile number associated with your CIBIL account. If you have forgotten your login credentials, use the Forgot Username or Forgot Password option available on the login page.

Step 2: Verify Your Identity with OTP

After entering your username and password and clicking Login, CIBIL will ask you to verify your identity.

An OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter the OTP in the required boxes and click Continue to access your CIBIL account.

If you do not receive the OTP, click on Resend OTP to request a new one.

💡
Tip:

Make sure you have access to the mobile number registered with your CIBIL account. If the registered mobile number is no longer active, you may need to update your details before completing the verification process.

Step 3: Open Your CIBIL Report

After successfully verifying the OTP, you will be logged in to your CIBIL account dashboard.

From the menu on the left-hand side, click on CIBIL Report. This will open your complete credit report,where you can review your loan accounts, credit cards, repayment history, enquiries, and other credit-related information.

💡
Tip:

Before raising a dispute, carefully review your complete CIBIL Report and identify the exact information that appears incorrect. This will help you select the correct account and dispute category in the next steps.

Step 4: Scroll Down and Click “Raise a Dispute”

After opening your CIBIL Report, scroll down to the bottom of the page. You will find the “Raise a Dispute” option in the footer section.

Click on “Raise a Dispute” to start the dispute process and report any incorrect information in your CIBIL report.

💡
Tip:

Before raising a dispute, carefully check your CIBIL report and identify the incorrect account, payment, balance, overdue amount, or personal information you want to dispute.

Step 5: Choose the Part of Your CIBIL Report You Want to Dispute

After clicking “Raise a Dispute”, you will be asked to choose which section of your CIBIL report contains the incorrect information.

You may see options such as:

🏦

Account Section

Loan, credit card, balance, payment history, account status, etc.

👤

Personal Information

Name, date of birth, gender, etc.

💼

Employment Information

Employment type, income, etc.

📋

Credit Application History

Incorrect loan or credit card enquiries.

📞

Contact Information

Address, mobile number, or other contact details.

ℹ️

Other Information

Other dispute-related issues.

For errors related to your loan or credit card, click “Account Section” to continue.

💡
Tip:

Choose the section carefully based on the information you want to correct. For incorrect loan, credit card, EMI, payment, overdue, or account status details, select Account Section.

Step 6: Find Your Issue and Click “Raise a Dispute”

After selecting the relevant issue from the Account Section, scroll down to read the available information and instructions related to that issue.

Once you reach the bottom of the page, you will see the “Raise a Dispute” button.

Click “Raise a Dispute” to proceed to the dispute form and submit your request for correction.

What to do:

Select the issue that matches your problem.

Scroll down and review the information provided by CIBIL.

At the bottom of the page, click “Raise a Dispute.”

Continue to the dispute form and provide the required details.

💡
Tip:

Before clicking “Raise a Dispute,” make sure you have selected the correct issue related to your loan or credit card account. Choosing the most relevant dispute reason can help ensure your request is sent for the appropriate review.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) ❓

The major payment systems used in India are UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, NACH (ACH/ECS), and Cheque. Each system serves a different purpose based on transaction value, speed, and frequency.

Both UPI and IMPS offer instant transfers 🟢. UPI is app-based and uses a UPI ID or QR code, while IMPS works through internet banking, mobile banking, and ATMs with higher limits.

Yes 😊. UPI is generally free for customers. Banks may charge merchants in some cases, but users usually do not pay any transaction fee.

For large-value transactions 💰, RTGS is the best option. It is designed for amounts above ₹2 lakh and settles transactions in real time with high security.

NEFT is a bank transfer system that settles payments in batches ⏱️. It is best used for routine transfers such as salaries, vendor payments, and regular bank transactions.

NACH (National Automated Clearing House) 🔁 is used for recurring and bulk payments such as loan EMIs, insurance premiums, salary credits, utility bills, and government subsidies.

NACH is the modern replacement for ECS. It offers faster processing, better success rates, and improved tracking compared to the older ECS system.

Yes 📝. Cheques are still used for legal, business, and formal transactions. They provide physical proof of payment but take longer to clear compared to digital methods.

UPI is the best choice for daily small payments 🛒. It is instant, easy to use, widely accepted, and works 24×7.

All major payment systems in India are regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), ensuring safety, transparency, and consumer protection.
