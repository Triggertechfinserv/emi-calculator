What We Do (And What We Don't)

✅ What We Do

  • Provide guidance-based loan eligibility assessment
  • Help users explore personal loans, home loans, flexi loans, and EMI reduction options
  • Suggest balance transfer or lower interest possibilities
  • Offer expert assistance before bank application

❌ What We Don't Do

  • We do not approve or reject loans
  • We do not guarantee interest rates or loan amounts
  • We do not perform CIBIL enquiries without your consent
  • We are not a bank or NBFC
